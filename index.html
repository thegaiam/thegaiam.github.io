The Generative AI Association of Malaysia

GAIAM is a community-driven association advancing the responsible adoption, research, and innovation of generative AI across Malaysia — connecting practitioners, researchers, businesses, and policymakers.

Get involved Learn more

About us

We bring together a diverse community across industry, academia, and the public sector to share knowledge, develop talent, and shape the future of generative AI in Malaysia. Our work emphasises ethics, inclusivity, and practical impact.

What we focus on

Community & Events

Meetups, workshops, and conferences that connect Malaysia's generative AI ecosystem.

Education & Talent

Training, mentorship, and resources to grow local capability in generative AI.

Research & Innovation

Supporting collaborative research and open knowledge-sharing across disciplines.

Responsible AI

Promoting ethical, safe, and inclusive practices in the deployment of generative AI.

Industry Adoption

Helping Malaysian businesses adopt generative AI productively and sustainably.

Policy Engagement

Engaging with policymakers on frameworks that enable trustworthy AI innovation.
